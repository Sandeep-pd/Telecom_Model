CONGESTION

Possible causes:
High traffic, limited network capacity, overloaded cell.

Symptoms:
High CPU, high traffic, reduced throughput and increased latency.

Recommended actions:
Check traffic distribution, perform load balancing, optimize neighboring cells and consider capacity expansion.


HIGH CPU

Possible causes:
Excessive traffic, software problems, routing problems or overloaded network equipment.

Symptoms:
High processor utilization and slower network processing.

Recommended actions:
Check running network services, inspect traffic and investigate overloaded equipment.


HIGH LATENCY

Possible causes:
Network congestion, backhaul problems, routing problems and poor network conditions.

Symptoms:
Slow response time and delayed communication.

Recommended actions:
Check congestion, inspect backhaul connectivity and investigate routing.


PACKET LOSS

Possible causes:
Network congestion, poor signal, faulty equipment and transport problems.

Symptoms:
Dropped packets, poor service quality and retransmissions.

Recommended actions:
Check signal quality, inspect network equipment and investigate transport connectivity.


LOW THROUGHPUT

Possible causes:
Poor signal strength, congestion, interference or limited network capacity.

Symptoms:
Slow data transfer and poor user experience.

Recommended actions:
Check radio conditions, traffic utilization and available capacity.
